Project

DashyNMS

Your LibreNMS, wherever you are.

A desktop and mobile client for LibreNMS. Sign in with your server address and an API token to see dashboards, devices and alerts, and get notified when something changes. It talks only to your own server.

  • Desktop Windows 10 and later · v1.0.0

    Dashboard, device grid and alert management from the system tray, with Windows notifications.

  • Mobile Beta iPhone and iPad · Android to follow

    Dashboard, Device View and alerts on your phone, with notifications and home screen widgets.
